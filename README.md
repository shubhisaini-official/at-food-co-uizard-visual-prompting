## 📌 Business Overview & Target Persona

### Brand Positioning
* **Brand Name:** AT Food Co.
* **Core Value Proposition:** Fresh, chef-prepared nutrition plans tailored for dogs, paired with a continuous digital *Dog Life Record* to deliver confidence, convenience, and continuity of care.
* **Category:** D2C Fresh Pet Wellness & Digital Health Tracking.

### Target Audience & Pain Points
* **Primary Persona:** Urban, busy pet parents (ages 25–45) who feel guilty feeding commercial dry kibble but lack time to cook fresh meals daily.
* **Core Friction Points:**
  1. **Health Guilt:** Concerns over ultra-processed kibble nutrient degradation and synthetic fillers.
  2. **Information Overload:** Conflicting nutrition advice across social media, forums, and peer groups regarding allergies and weight management.
  3. **Operational Fatigue:** Cooking balanced fresh pet food requires hours of meal prep, storage space, and complex caloric math.

## 🔄 CRO Copy Engineering & Prompt Critique Logs

To transform baseline AI copy into high-converting, emotion-driven messaging, an iterative CRO critique sequence was executed.

### Phase 1: Initial Copy Generation Prompt

> Act as a world-class conversion rate optimization (CRO) copywriter for a DTC pet wellness startup called "AT Food Co."
> 
> Product: Fresh, chef-prepared dog food subscriptions paired with a digital "Dog Life Record" health tracker.
> Target Audience: Urban dog parents who feel guilty about feeding kibble but lack time to cook fresh meals daily.
> 
> Generate 3 conversion-focused variations for:
> 1. Main Headline (Clear, benefit-driven, max 10 words)
> 2. Sub-headline (Solves the primary pain point, max 20 words)
> 3. Primary Call-To-Action (CTA) Button Text (Action-oriented, low friction)

**Raw AI Output:**
* **Headline:** "Real Fresh Food for Your Dog. Zero Guesswork for You."
* **Sub-headline:** "Chef-prepared, vet-approved fresh meal plans delivered weekly to your doorstep, backed by a digital health record tailored to your dog."
* **CTA Button:** "Build Your Dog's Fresh Plan"

---

### Phase 2: Aggressive CRO Critique & Optimization Prompt

> Critique the copy above from the perspective of an aggressive CRO expert.
> 1. Identify 3 weak words or vague promises that lower conversion intent.
> 2. Rewrite the Headline, Sub-headline, and CTA to increase emotional resonance, urgency, and perceived value.
> 3. Add a micro-copy trust signal below the CTA button.

**CRO Expert Diagnostic Analysis:**
* **Weakness Identified in Headline:** *"Real Fresh Food"* is passive and generic. Lacks a direct verb or emotional hook addressing feeding anxiety.
* **Weakness Identified in CTA:** *"Build Your Dog's Fresh Plan"* creates high cognitive friction (sounds like effort).
* **Fix Applied:** Replaced passive claims with an active command (*"Stop Guessing..."*) and anchored the CTA to a high-value trial offer (*"Get 50% Off Your First Trial Box"*). Added explicit risk-reduction micro-copy directly below the button.

## 🗄️ Backend Airtable Lead Database Schema (`AT_Food_Co_Leads`)

| Field Name | Field Type | Description & Field Options |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lead_ID** | Autonumber | Unique record identifier (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`) |
| **Full_Name** | Single line text | Name of the pet parent |
| **Email_Address** | Email | Contact email for trial delivery |
| **Dog_Breed** | Single line text | Breed of dog (*Golden Retriever, Indie, Beagle*) |
| **Dog_Age_Category** | Single Select | `Puppy (<1 yr)`, `Adult (1-7 yrs)`, `Senior (7+ yrs)` |
| **Submission_Timestamp** | Created time | Automated record creation date/time |
| **Status** | Single Select | `New Lead`, `Trial Sent`, `Contacted`, `Converted` |

## 🔌 Technical Form Integration & Workflow Automation

1. **Frontend Construction:** Built in Softr/Framer connected natively to Airtable via Personal Access Token (PAT) API authentication.
2. **Field Mapping Logic:**
   * `Form Input: Parent Name` mapped to `Full_Name`
   * `Form Input: Parent Email` mapped to `Email_Address`
   * `Form Input: Dog Breed` mapped to `Dog_Breed`
3. **Automated Conversion Redirect:** Upon form submission, the system triggers a real-time row insertion into Airtable and instantly redirects the user to a custom confirmation screen (*"Thank you! Check your inbox for your fresh trial code."*).
